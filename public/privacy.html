Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: GameHub has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no database. We keep your room's data only while it is needed, and we never sell anything about you.

1. What we don't collect

We don't ask for your email, phone number, password, location, camera or contacts. We don't use cookies, advertising or analytics.

2. What the server keeps while you play

InformationWhyHow long
The name you type, which doesn't need to be your real nameTo show it to the other playersOnly in the server's temporary memory. It is never saved to a database. It is deleted when the room closes: about 30 minutes after everyone has disconnected, or as soon as everyone has left the lobby. A server restart also erases it.
A random player ID created by your browserTo put you back in your seat if you reconnect
Chat messages, sentences, drawings, votes and scoresTo run the game and show them to your room

Everything you post is visible to the other players in your room. Please don't post private information. Drawings and chat from a finished game may stay in memory until the host starts another game or the room closes.

3. What is saved on your own device

Your browser keeps a few small items so the site remembers you. They stay on your device and are not cookies:

ItemWhat it remembers
gh_nameThe name you last used
gh_tokenYour random player ID
gh_roomYour current room code, so you can rejoin
gh_theme, gh_mute, ch_modeLight or dark mode, sound on or off, and your Dumb Charades setting

Your browser also saves a copy of the site's files so it opens quickly and Dumb Charades works offline. You can remove all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

4. Outside services

Like any website, GameHub relies on a few other companies. When your browser or our server connects to them, they may receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • Render hosts the site and may keep technical server logs. The servers may be outside your country. See Render's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Wikimedia supplies player photos in IPL Auction. See Wikimedia's privacy policy.

5. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services above or where the law requires it.

If you use a Share button, such as for a Doodle Telephone story, your device's share menu opens and you choose where it goes. A story image shows players' names and what they wrote and drew. It is created on your device, and we don't keep a copy of it.

6. Security

The live site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No website can promise perfect security, and anyone with your room code can join your lobby, so only share it with people you trust.

7. Your choices

You can use any name you like, leave a room at any time, and clear the data saved on your device. Because rooms are temporary and aren't linked to an account, we normally can't look up past activity. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as asking to see or delete it. You can contact us about this below.

8. Children

GameHub is not designed for children under 13, who should not use it. If you are under 18, please use it with the permission of a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect information about children beyond what is described above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GameHub changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue on our GitHub page. Issues there are public, so please don't include private information.
